    Questions

    What exactly is a credit?

    One unit of agent work. A Quick run costs 1 credit, a Standard run 3, a Deep run 9 — and the cost is printed on the run button before you press it. Credits never expire and nothing renews.

    Which models am I actually getting?

    Quick runs on Claude Haiku 4.5, Standard on Claude Sonnet 5, and Deep on Claude Opus 5. The tier you pick is the model you get — it's listed on each tier card.

    What if a run comes back empty or fails?

    The credits go straight back to your balance and the refund appears in your credit history. You only pay for runs that produce something.

    Do I need an account or password?

    No. Buying credits issues an access key. Paste it here and your balance loads; that's the whole login. Lost it? Request a new one by email and the old key retires.

    Is this the same as the free tool on the storefront?

    No. Watch AI do your task demonstrates a product from the catalog on your example. The studio works on your job with the strongest models, multiple research steps, and a saved library of deliverables.

    Can I still buy the one-off tools?

    Yes — the catalog is unchanged. Credits are for work you want done now; the packs and blueprints are for workflows you want to own and repeat.

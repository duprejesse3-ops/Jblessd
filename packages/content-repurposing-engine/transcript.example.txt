Host: Welcome back to the show. Today we're talking with Priya about how her
team cut new-hire onboarding from six weeks down to four days, just by
rewriting their internal docs.

Priya: Yeah, so the big insight was that nobody was reading the docs because
they were written for the person who wrote them, not the person who needed
them. We rebuilt everything around "what do you need to do right now" instead
of "here is everything about this system."

Host: Did you use any tools for that, or was it all just rewriting?

Priya: Honestly ninety percent of it was just rewriting. We did add a search
bar, but the actual unlock was ruthlessly cutting anything that wasn't a step
someone needed to take today. If it was background info, we moved it to an
appendix nobody has to read on day one.

Host: What was the reaction internally when you shipped it?

Priya: A little skepticism at first — people had spent years on the old docs.
But the first new hire who went through it finished setup in a day and a
half, and after that nobody argued.

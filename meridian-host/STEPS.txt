SKU AI-HOST-001

You bring your GitHub, your Stripe, your machine.
Not a copy of the MultiNicheAI catalog.

0. chmod +x multinicheai *.sh
1. ./multinicheai from-github https://github.com/YOU/YOUR-SHOP.git
2. Keys in container.env — never Git.
3. sudo ./multinicheai bootstrap shop.yourname.com
4. Pi: 64-bit Pi OS, Pi 4 4GB or Pi 5.
5. sudo ./multinicheai forward shop.yourname.com  (or ./multinicheai share)
6. Stripe webhook https://shop.yourname.com/api/webhook — test 4242 before moving the main name.
